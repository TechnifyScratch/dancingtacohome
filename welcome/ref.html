Welcome to Dancing Taco.

Look in the bottom-right corner of this tab. That’s where Carlos dances by default.

How it works See what’s next

Why isn’t Carlos on the Chrome Web Store?

Chrome blocks extensions from modifying its store pages for security. Carlos appears on regular webpages where the extension has permission to run.

If you don’t see him here, refresh this tab, then check that Dancing Taco is enabled and has access to this site.

Make yourself at home

Open Chrome’s Extensions menu and select Dancing Taco. Pin it for quick access from the toolbar.

Pick a corner

Move Carlos to any of the four corners from the extension’s settings.

Adjust his size

Use the size slider to make Carlos bigger or smaller.

Give him a break

Turn off the Enabled switch to hide him, then switch it back on when you want him around again.
