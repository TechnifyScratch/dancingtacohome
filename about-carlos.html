About Carlos

Meet the mustachioed taco who dances in the corner of your browser.

The idea

Put a tiny taco in the corner of a webpage and give him a dance. That’s the whole premise of Dancing Taco, a Chrome extension built around a character named Carlos.

He fits into your browser

Carlos can dance in whichever corner you pick. You can make him bigger, make him smaller, or hide him until you want him back. See his settings.
